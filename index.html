Hi, I’m Zoe!

Bread n’ Buttah is my little one-person bakery. I make every single thing myself — from feeding the sourdough starter to shaping each loaf and packing up your order.

No factory, no big ovens, just real ingredients, a home kitchen, and a lot of love (and flour everywhere). Thanks so much for supporting a small local baker. 💛

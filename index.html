— About me —

Hi there!

Welcome to Bread N’ Buttah!

I’m a 20-year-old with a love for all things homemade, cozy, and comforting.

Growing up in Morocco, freshly baked bread was a part of everyday life. It was always there, warm and homemade, something I never thought twice about. But when I moved to Bend, Oregon, I realized just how special those simple moments were and how much they reminded me of home.

Wanting to bring a little piece of that home with me, I taught myself the art of sourdough baking. What started as a simple passion soon grew into a little dream of sharing that same warmth and comfort with others.

And that’s how Bread N’ Buttah came to life!

Every loaf of sourdough and batch of butter is made from scratch, in small batches, with care and love. My hope is that each bite feels like a little piece of home, because I believe the simplest things, made with love, are often the most meaningful.

Thank you for supporting my little dream. I’m so happy to share a piece of my home with yours.
